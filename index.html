Who Can Nominate?

Recognition works both ways across every level of TD&E.

RoleCan NominateCan Be Nominated
Any TD&E Employee✔✔
Manager✔✔
Director / Area Director+✔✔
Customer (via a TD&E employee)✔ (Customer Delight only)—
SR / PMO Contractor / Contingent Worker—✔ (Customer Delight only)

Nomination rights vary a lot by award: the TD&E Award and BIG Thank You are both manager-submitted, and the five Strategic Resourcing MVPs (SEM, RM, DST, SPA, SRX/GSPM) are nominated internally by leadership with no open form. Interns aren't explicitly addressed in current program guidelines — check with TD&E Strategic Initiatives before nominating one. See each award's card for its specific rule.

Specific stories win. Here's the framework.
